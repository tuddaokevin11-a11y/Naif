Rules and Regulations


  • Wear proper dance attire and footwear.
  • Arrive on time for classes and practice.
  • Respect instructors, staff, and fellow dancers.
  • Keep the studio clean and organized.
  • Warm up before dancing to prevent injuries.
  • Use studio equipment properly and carefully.
  • Ask permission before recording other dancers.
  • Follow all studio policies and safety instructions.

Dance with passion. Practice with discipline. Respect everyone.
